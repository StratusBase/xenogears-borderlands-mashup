# Fire quality target

Approved direction: hybrid A+B, with C on actual explosive detonations. Preserve the authored forked silhouettes approved in the A+C proof; use a small supporting volume, curved animated contours, independently bending tips and a restrained hot core. Large impact art belongs to primary hits. Secondary recipients get smaller exposure-weighted embers, marks and flamelets.

- **A / Flashover:** short white/gold contact, directional orange forks, warm red rim, coherent interiors, tip-first breakup. Choose an independent cosmetic event profile once per impact. Six nonrepeating bearing sectors produce left/right and shallow/steep upward forks; the profile also varies family, bend, phase and timing without consuming combat randomness. Floor profiles lean around upward flow, while ceiling profiles retain shallow left/right rolls. Impact embers share the chosen handedness; projectile travel direction remains authoritative.
- **B / Cinderbrand:** localized soot and heat crawling over the actual visible material. Cloth carries softer char and scattered embers; armor retains readable panels and tighter hot seams. Skin receives only a subtle stylized response. No gore, deformation or melting.
- **Lingering flames:** curved uneven lower contours, roots scattered in two surface directions, different starts, tilt and height. Follow the physical source and clip behind its support plane. Existing status intensity and lifetime control the body response. World wisps cool cosmetically; they create no damaging hazard or new status.
- **C / Backdraft:** open, offset asymmetric crescents from the actual detonation event, with stronger independent bends, interior heat and a shorter reach. Contact and fuse remain separate. Nearby recipients must not repeat the primary explosion silhouette. Authoritative Cluster bomblets use a smaller primary burst and quieter pressure cue; their casings, fuses and classic delivery remain simulation-owned.
- **World supports:** impact forks rise from floors, remain readable on walls/slopes, and roll shallowly along ceilings. Underside flamelets travel along their support instead of hanging down. The existing support-plane clip rejects hidden fragments.
- **Equipment:** cooled heat-transfer bands and cartridge chambers carry local hot seams. A physical feed duct and angled dark vent shoe clear the barrel shell; recessed ember baffles define its opening. No idle tears. After a real shot, short crossed authored tears escape from the firing cartridge and cool. Pilot idle-to-fire contrast is deliberate; delivery and heavy/compact construction govern the cue. Explicit socket indices select the firing mech arm. Preserve receiver paint, grip, magazine, optics, equipment slots, muzzle positions and original materials. See [fire-equipment.md](fire-equipment.md) for the Opus-guided alignment and verification.
- **Flight:** retain delivery-specific physical casings. Replace Fire cones with deforming crossed authored tears. Sparse broken wakes distinguish small rounds from energetic payloads; beam precision retains its exact endpoints and a fine core. Every unrelated elemental channel remains owned by its own module.
- **Audio:** a short ignition transient, delivery-dependent air rush, quiet equipment hiss, granular burn crackle and a heavier detonation release. Use the existing physical/elemental buses, attenuation, voice caps, pause, unlock and recording taps. Never amplify the idle loop to compete with weapon reports.
