## Independent finding

The initial branching-lightning revision was clearly closer than `b2dd1ec`, but still below the boards. Its white cores, blue glow, branches and dominant lash read as lightning; the previous rods and broad sawtooth ribbons did not. Claude identified these remaining weaknesses:

1. Lingering charge resembled cracked glass: equal-width straight segments from a common center, without distinct bright islands or sparse aftermath.
2. Additive blue washed into pale lavender against the desert. A normal-blended cobalt separation layer should sit beneath the additive core.
3. Heavy hits lacked C's hot onset and readable directional spark streaks; the main arc bowed like a rubber band.
4. Fixed angle/length families and a 97-value serial seed made hits repeat. Armor/cloth differed mainly in line width.
5. Long cosmetic leaders could be mistaken for a chain to another character. Surface charge did not crawl over finite edges.
