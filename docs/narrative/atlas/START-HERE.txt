THEN, MACHINES MADE GODS — STORY ATLAS v0.1

Open machines-made-gods-atlas.html in a browser.
The file works offline and includes the whole proposed story.

• Story spine: condensed causal history through the ending.
• History: the actual historical sequence.
• Campaign: the player’s discoveries, actions, and consequences.
• Cast: protagonists, antagonists, and their roles, motives, and fates.
• World: gods, machines, artifacts, places, and factions.
• Open questions: decisions we can record as we revise.

Select a card, then choose Edit card. Drag cards to rearrange the map.
Use + Card and + Arrow to change its structure. Causal loops are rejected.
Arrows mean causation. Dotted entity connections mean association.

Browser edits are kept locally when supported. They do not automatically
overwrite this file. Choose Save editable atlas to download a portable revision
with your current story embedded. Save story data downloads editable JSON.
Open revision imports either a saved atlas or its JSON.

Only the three-sentence hook is agreed. All other content is proposed.
The story is original; the reference games are influences, not shared canon.

For future workspace edits: story.json is the workspace master. The HTML embeds
its own copy. Import newer exported story data before rebuilding from _source.
