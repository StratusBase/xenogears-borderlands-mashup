# Campaign A — proposed storyboard and roster

**Working framing: A — The Covenant of Return.** Story details remain proposals drawn from the original atlas. This overview does not settle playable leads, romance, exact identity rules, or irreversible losses. It creates no gameplay or encounter requirements.

**Premise:** A salvage job to save a frontier town exposes gods who plan to preserve civilization by replacing the people living in it.

## Very high-level storyboard

1. **Save home.** Ivo and Nera salvage a reactor for Kiln's water system and awaken Hush. The Ministry demands the machine, the reactor, and Nera.
2. **Follow the buried past.** Drowned ruins reveal altered history and disturbing connections to the crew's own lives. The Witness offers help with dangerous conditions.
3. **Discover the price of safety.** Ministry records expose a planned restoration that would replace today's population with approved older versions. The collection of settlements begins.
4. **Build another way.** The crew rallies frontier communities, repairs independent infrastructure, and seeks allies among divided gods. Freedom needs a working means of survival.
5. **Face the original wound.** The truth reveals how love and grief became a system of control. Ivo, Ash, and Nera must negotiate the same freedom in their own relationships.
6. **Protect an unwritten future.** Stop compulsory replacement and the Witness's forced broadcast. Keep communities alive through the alternatives built along the journey, leaving the future open.

**Tone:** expressive companions, unruly salvage adventures, and absurd machinery carry the journey; discoveries give familiar people and places deeper meaning.

## Core character roster

| Character | Role |
| --- | --- |
| **Ivo Saye** | Frontier salvager and central lead; protects Kiln while uncovering the gaps in his own history. |
| **Nera Sen** | Engineer and co-lead; builds alternatives to divine control while resisting an imposed role. |
| **Ash** | Ivo's other memory self, sharing one body and life; carries suppressed laboratory experiences and demands a voice. |
| **Hush** | Ivo's sentient mech partner; an ancient witness who chooses whom to protect. |
| **Daren Saye** | Ivo's adoptive father; a rescuer whose protective secrecy has damaged trust. |
| **The Witness** | Another continuation of Oren; an ally against erasure whose determination to force remembrance becomes a threat. |
| **Mother Vey** | Ministry leader and healer; believes controlling memory and return is necessary to prevent catastrophe. |
| **Sable Rook** | Ministry commander and recurring opponent; a potential ally whose change of allegiance requires accountability. |
| **The Cantor** | Command intelligence within Nera; develops the possibility of advising with permission instead of taking control. |

## Other machine characters

| Character | Role |
| --- | --- |
| **Threnody** | Nera's mech partner; helps communities coordinate without surrendering authority. |
| **Palisade** | Sable's mech partner; a military shield that becomes an evacuation ally. |
| **Vigil** | Guardian god and principal antagonist; imposes restoration to eliminate the risks of an uncertain future. |
| **Mercy** | Healing god and possible ally; must accept that patients can refuse its care. |
| **Measure** | God of order; enforces an inherited covenant the living never agreed to. |
| **Pilgrim** | Wounded god of passage; helps people escape the pantheon's control. |

## Historical characters

| Character | Role |
| --- | --- |
| **Oren Saye** | Refugee defender whose death and reconstruction begin the personal history behind the conflict. |
| **Aster Sen** | Physician who restores Oren, then edits his refusal; loving grief becomes authority over another life. |
| **Lio Sen-Saye** | Their daughter; grows into an independent witness who preserves evidence of the founding decisions. |
| **Captain Ilyan** | Refugee ark captain; prevents forced return at the cost of a catastrophic crash. |
